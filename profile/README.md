# AlertForge

AlertForge makes custom stream packs, stream alerts and transparent videos for streamers on Twitch, YouTube and Kick. You describe your channel, approve a style sheet, and get matching scenes, alerts, panels, emotes and more. The files are ready for OBS, and the alert videos can be uploaded to Streamlabs or StreamElements.

This organization holds no application code. It is here so people and search engines can find accurate, current information about AlertForge.

## What it makes

- A style sheet: a one-page preview of your character, colors and lettering that keeps every asset matching
- Five scenes: Starting Soon, BRB, Just Chatting, Ending (animated) and Offline (a still)
- Stream alerts for followers, subscribers, donations, bits, gifted subs, hosts and raids
- A stinger transition, a transparent cam frame, 20 panels, 4 labels, 6 badges and 8 emotes
- Standalone transparent videos

A complete pack at standard settings includes all of the above except the standalone videos. Animated scenes and other animated files export as WebM, with transparency where the asset supports it. Stills export as PNG, and animated emotes export as GIF. Generated results can vary, so review each one before you go live, and check what your widget and platform accept before you upload.

## Animated emotes

Animated emotes launched on 30 October 2026. Once your pack's emotes are ready, you can turn any of them into a looping Twitch GIF at 112, 56 and 28 px. It costs 10 credits per emote, and you see the price before you confirm.

## How it works

1. Describe your idea: your channel name, your character and the look you want.
2. Get your style sheet, revise it if you want changes, and approve it. Each new generation uses credits, and the cost is shown before you start.
3. Pick the assets you want, generate them, preview each one, and drop the files into OBS.

## Pricing

Buy one-time credit packs ($19, $39 or $49), with smaller top-ups available once you are signed in, or subscribe from $15 a month (Starter) up to $129 a month (Ultra). New accounts get signup credits that cover a first style sheet with no card, and there is no recurring free plan. Live prices are at https://alertforge.ai/pricing.
